# Moodify Android: Changelog

## 1.1.0 (polish round, November 2026)

### Fixed
- **Search filters:**
  - A filter no longer shows the previous filter's results.
  - Album, artist and playlist results are listed top to bottom.
  - Clearing a search, or tapping a recent search, resets the filter to All.
  - The top result isn't repeated in Songs.
  - Duplicate songs and podcast episodes are removed.
- **Search box:**
  - Tapping into existing text puts the cursor at the end.
  - Closing the keyboard returns to your results.
- **Artists:** tapping an artist's name opens their page, which now shows their real photo. "Go to album" is new.
- **Song subtitles in playlists** no longer show the song's length ("Dire Straits - 4 minutes, 27 seconds").
- **Accessibility:**
  - Readable text on the mood tiles.
  - Library tiles grow with large fonts instead of cutting text off.
  - TalkBack no longer reaches the screen behind the full player.
- **Clearing listening history** now also clears the play counts behind For you.

### New
- **Moods:** one mood system everywhere. Each mood opens its own page with songs and YouTube's mood playlists for your region.
- **Discover previews:** tap "Hear previews" to hear a short clip of each card. They never interrupt your own music. Undo brings back the last card you swiped.
- **Library:**
  - Save albums and YouTube playlists, and follow artists.
  - New filter chips: All, Playlists, Albums, Artists.
  - Home shows "From your library" and "Recently played" rows.
- **Player:**
  - "Playing from…" shows where the music came from.
  - Save to playlist is now one of the player's main buttons.
  - The sleep timer and equaliser are in the ⋯ menu.
  - Speed and pitch sit behind one "Playback speed" row.
- **Settings:**
  - Searchable country and language pickers.
  - New options: Data saver, Skip silence, Download on Wi-Fi only, Download liked songs automatically, and Pause listening history.
  - A Storage section with Clear buttons for each kind of saved data.
  - Back up and restore your library to a file.
- **Your listening:** your top songs and artists, total time listened, and a way to share them.
- **Open YouTube and YouTube Music links in Moodify**, either directly or with Share → Moodify.
- **Offline lyrics:** lyrics download along with each song.
- **Crash log:** the last crash report is kept on your phone (Settings › Advanced). It is shared only if you choose to share it.

### Changed
- **Home:**
  - A slimmer header, so music shows on the first screen.
  - More varied rows.
  - YouTube chips no longer repeat moods or include podcasts.
- **Song menu:** Play next and Add to queue come first.
- **Main buttons** use the Moodify accent on every page.
- **Library tiles** are calmer.
- **Every tab is labelled.** Tapping the tab you're on scrolls back to the top.
- **Release builds are signed** with the Moodify key.
  - If you're switching from an older debug build, uninstall it once first. Android won't update an app that's signed with a different key.
  - Back up your library with Back up & restore before uninstalling.
