05 / product guardrails

Three rules for the next build

Do not flatten catalog data before rendering.Sections and endpoints are product meaning. Preserve them through the API contract.
Keep the mood layer editorial, not fake.Moodify can own the language, color, and atmosphere while InnerTube supplies live music inventory.
Make ArchiveTune the catalog reference, not the product roadmap.Adopt its proven discovery spine, but keep Moodify focused on feeling, taste, and social context.
